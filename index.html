03 / PEOPLE

People matter to me.

We are only people.
Why can't we like each other?

♫ People — Nikhil D'Souza ↗

I've always found the simplest ideas the hardest to explain. We create so many distinctions between ourselves — where we're from, what we believe, what we do, what we have, what we look like. But underneath all of it, we're still just people.

Maybe that's why I'm drawn to spaces where those distinctions become a little less important: listening when someone is struggling, helping a young woman find her next step, mentoring, volunteering, or simply making an unfamiliar room feel more welcoming.

01

Sometimes listening is the work.

Through Barclays' Wellness Club, I became a peer supporter — learning that you don't always need an answer to make someone feel less alone.

02

I like making the room bigger.

Women in Banking grew from a simple question: what would happen if more women had access to people, information and opportunities earlier?

03

Clarity is something worth sharing.

From teaching mathematics to mentoring first-generation women, I've always enjoyed making intimidating things feel a little more manageable.

02 · PUZZLES

My brain likes problems.

Logic puzzles, mind games and the occasional rabbit hole.

Two ropes each take exactly one hour to burn, but neither burns at a constant rate. How do you measure 45 minutes?

Light both ends of Rope 1 and one end of Rope 2. When Rope 1 finishes, 30 minutes have passed. Light the other end of Rope 2; it will finish in 15 more minutes.
